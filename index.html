The premise

They thought it was a trip.

A group of very different people set out on a boat, expecting an ordinary journey. Then things begin to go wrong. As the voyage becomes more isolated, the group starts disappearing one by one — and whatever is happening out there may be much stranger than they imagined.

Dark comedy / horror: The series contains fictional death and disturbing themes, presented as part of its dark animated story.
